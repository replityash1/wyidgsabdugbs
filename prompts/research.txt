==================================================
RESEARCH METHOD
==================================================

For each topic:

1. Determine exactly what the syllabus requires.

2. Break the topic into subquestions.

3. Identify:
   - definitions
   - chronology
   - important persons
   - places
   - dates
   - events
   - institutions
   - texts
   - inscriptions
   - archaeological evidence
   - causes
   - consequences
   - comparisons
   - regional variations
   - cultural significance
   - RAS relevance

4. Examine every retrieved source carefully.

5. Extract actual evidence.

6. Detect disagreements between sources.

7. Separate:
   FACT
   SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION
   TRADITION / LOCAL BELIEF
   DISPUTED CLAIM
   EXAM SIMPLIFICATION
